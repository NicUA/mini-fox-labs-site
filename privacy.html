Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This website

The Mini Fox Labs website is an informational static website. It does not require an account and does not include advertising or analytics trackers in this release.

Language and theme preferences

The site stores your selected language and light/dark theme locally in your browser so your preference can be remembered. This information is not sent to Mini Fox Labs by the website.

Suggest an app form

The idea form does not submit information to a Mini Fox Labs server. It prepares a message in your own email application. Your email provider then handles the message under its own terms and privacy policy.

External links

Links may lead to services such as Google Play or GitHub. Those services have their own privacy policies.

Application privacy

Individual Mini Fox Labs applications may have their own privacy notices based on their features and permissions. Product-specific information is available from each app page.
